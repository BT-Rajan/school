# Training material

One short guide per role, in English and Arabic. Each covers signing in, the screens the role
uses, the everyday tasks step by step, and where to get help. They name buttons and pages as the
app shows them, so a trainer can walk through them on the staging server with the browser-test
accounts.

| Role | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| District Admin | [district-admin.md](en/district-admin.md) | [district-admin.md](ar/district-admin.md) |
| Form Author | [form-author.md](en/form-author.md) | [form-author.md](ar/form-author.md) |
| Cluster Head | [cluster-head.md](en/cluster-head.md) | [cluster-head.md](ar/cluster-head.md) |
| Supervisor | [supervisor.md](en/supervisor.md) | [supervisor.md](ar/supervisor.md) |
| Teacher | [teacher.md](en/teacher.md) | [teacher.md](ar/teacher.md) |

Suggested sessions: District Admins and Form Authors 2 hours; Cluster Heads 1 hour; supervisors
1.5 hours with their phones (installing the app, a scan, working offline); teachers 20 minutes,
or the guide alone.
