# For everyone: signing in and finding your way

**Signing in.** Open the address the district gave you. Enter your email and password, then the
6-digit code: either the one sent to your email, or the one in your authenticator app (Google
Authenticator or similar). Admins and Form Authors always use an authenticator app.

- Lost your phone? Use one of your recovery codes ("Use a recovery code instead"). Each works
  once. No codes left? Ask a District Admin to reset your sign-in method.
- Five wrong codes lock the account for 15 minutes. An admin can unlock it sooner.
- You are signed out after 12 hours without activity.

**Language.** The English / العربية button at the top of every screen switches at once,
without losing what you have typed. Your choice is saved to your account.

**Notifications.** The bell shows new notices. On the Notifications page you choose, for each
kind of notice, whether it comes in the app, by email (in a daily summary) or as a push to this
device.

**My account.** Change your password, switch between emailed codes and an authenticator app,
make new recovery codes, and see the devices you are signed in on (sign any of them out).

**Install the app.** On a phone, open the site in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) and choose
"Add to Home screen". It then opens like an app, and works without a connection for the screens
you have opened before.

**Help.** Ask your District Admin. They can look at your screens with you, read only, if you
agree it would help; you will see a notice the next time you sign in.
