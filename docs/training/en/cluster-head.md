# Cluster Head

Read [For everyone](common.md) first. You look after your cluster: its schools, teachers and
supervisors, who visits which school, and the teachers' parts of evaluations.

## Every week

1. **Home**: schools without a supervisor or a contact teacher, evaluation progress by school,
   each supervisor's workload, overdue teacher requests, and reports shared with you.
2. **Assignments**: **Assign** a supervisor to a school with a start date, **Reassign** to move a
   school to another supervisor, or end an assignment. If two people change the same school at
   once, the second is told and shown the latest state. When a supervisor leaves a school with
   draft evaluations, you choose: hand the drafts to the new supervisor or cancel them.
3. **Schools**: each school's contact teacher (the teacher who answers the school's part of
   evaluations). Change it when the contact changes; requests not yet started move to the new
   contact, started ones ask you first.

## Teacher requests and evaluations

- **Teacher requests** lists the requests in your cluster and their due dates. A teacher who is
  away can be excused: **Waive** their part with a reason, so the supervisor can submit.
- You can send a request yourself on forms an admin approved for it: choose the schools or the
  teachers, a due date and instructions.
- **Evaluations** shows your cluster's evaluations; open one to read it or download its PDF.

## Reports

- **Scanned reports → Forwarded to me** lists the reports an admin shared with you. Copies you
  open carry your name and the time.
- **Standard reports** for your cluster, on screen or as Excel or PDF.
