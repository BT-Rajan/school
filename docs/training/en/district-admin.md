# District Admin

Read [For everyone](common.md) first. You run the district's system: its structure, the people
in it, the ministry's forms, and every scanned report. There are always at least two admins.

## Every day

1. **Home** shows the district at a glance: counts, the report inbox, coverage of each open
   ministry report, forms and their windows, the audit check, mail use, storage and
   quarantined files. Anything that needs you is in amber.
2. **Scanned reports** is your inbox. Open a report to read it (it opens watermarked with your
   name). Then **Acknowledge** it, or **Return to supervisor** with what needs to change. The
   other admin sees who did what. **Share with the Cluster Head** lets the school's Cluster Head
   read it; you can stop sharing later.

## The district

- **Clusters**, **Schools**, **Teachers**, **Supervisors**: add, edit, transfer, deactivate.
  Nothing is ever deleted; deactivated records stay and can be reactivated.
- **Import and export**: load schools, teachers and supervisors from Excel or CSV. Download the
  template, fill it in English or Arabic headings, preview, then commit. Teacher invitations go
  out a little each day to stay within the mail limit; the dates are shown.
- **Admins and Cluster Heads**: invite staff, unlock accounts, **Reset sign-in method** for
  someone who lost their authenticator (also on the Teachers and Supervisors pages).
- **Reference lists** and **Custom fields**: the values and extra fields used on forms and
  records, in both languages.

## Forms and scores

1. Form Authors build forms and **Send for review**. Open one under **Forms**, check it in both
   languages, then **Publish** with its start and end dates, or **Return to author** with a
   comment. A published form's content cannot change; you can extend its end date or close it
   early, with a reason.
2. **Scoring** (from the form's page): the district's formula as rules. Enter the worked
   examples the district agreed, with the result each should give. **Activate** is possible
   only when every example matches. Activating new rules changes no score already given; use
   **Recalculate scores** with a reason when they should change. Every change is recorded.

## Reports and exports

- **Standard reports**: evaluation status by school, supervisor or month; teacher request
  completion; assignment history; raw answers of a form; scanned reports. Filter, look on
  screen, and export to Excel or PDF in English or Arabic. Every export is recorded.
- **Download scanned reports as a ZIP**: up to 200 reports matching the filters, each
  watermarked with your name, made in the background.

## Administration

- **Settings**: the district's name, logo, time zone, languages, sign-in rules, mail, retention,
  upload limits and notification defaults. Locked accounts and recent failed sign-ins are at the
  bottom.
- **Audit log**: everything that happened, searchable by who, what, on what and when. Exporting it
  is itself recorded.
- **Support**: find a person, then **View their screens** (read only, with a reason, up to
  30 minutes; they are told) or **Export their data** for an access request.

The [operations runbooks](../../runbooks.md) cover backups, restores, emergencies and incidents.
