# Supervisor

Read [For everyone](common.md) first. You visit your assigned schools, fill in evaluations, and
send scanned reports to the District Admins. Install the app on your phone: it works without a
connection.

## An evaluation

1. **Evaluations → New evaluation**: choose the form (only open ones are listed), the school,
   and for a teacher evaluation the teacher. Set the visit date.
2. Answer the questions. **Save draft** keeps your answers; you can come back any time while the
   form is open.
3. If the form has a teacher's part, it goes to the school's contact teacher (or the teacher
   being evaluated). You can submit once they have submitted, or the Cluster Head has excused
   them. If you need changes, return it to them with a comment.
4. **Submit**. The score is worked out by the system. To correct a submitted evaluation, use
   **Amend** with a reason; the earlier answers are kept.
5. Download the PDF in English or Arabic.

## A scanned report

1. **Scanned reports → Upload a report**: choose the ministry report and version, the school,
   the report date (and the teacher, for a teacher report).
2. **Scan with camera**: take each page; the app finds the page's edges and straightens it. Drag
   the corners if needed, turn a page, choose black and white for faint copies, reorder or remove
   pages, then **Use these pages**. You can also add photos or PDFs from the phone.
3. **Upload and send**. The admins are told. You see when it was viewed, acknowledged, or
   returned with a comment; for a return, upload a new version and send it again.

## Without a connection

Start evaluations, save answers, submit, and scan reports as usual. The header shows
"Waiting: N"; **Waiting to send** lists what is on the phone. It is sent by itself when you are
back online, once, in order. If someone changed the same evaluation meanwhile, you choose which
version to keep. Keep the app installed, and do not sign out with items waiting: signing out
erases them from the phone.

**My dashboard** shows your reports, the returned ones, and for each open ministry report which
of your schools still have none.
