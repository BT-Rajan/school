# Teacher

Read [For everyone](common.md) first. You answer the parts of evaluations and requests that are
for teachers. You see only your own requests.

1. You get an email when a request arrives, and reminders before and on its due date.
2. **My requests** lists what is waiting, with due dates. Open one.
3. Read the instructions, answer the questions, and **Save draft** as you go. You can come back
   to it.
4. **Submit** when it is complete. If the requester returns it with a comment, change what they
   ask and **Submit again**.
5. The school's contact teacher also answers the school's part of school evaluations; these appear
   in the same list.
